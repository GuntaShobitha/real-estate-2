Frequently Asked Questions

Advisory Protocol & Escrow

Essential answers regarding confidentiality, non-resident purchasing, and legal escrow security.

How does Stackly maintain client confidentiality and discretion?
+
All private client interactions are protected under stringent non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). Off-market transactions are executed via bespoke private holding entities with direct escrow handling by our specialized legal concierge.
What is the process for scheduling a private in-person or virtual 3D tour?
+
Verified clients can schedule private viewings directly through the portal or via their assigned private client advisor. We provide 24/7 private aviation transfers and secure biometric showings.
Can international non-resident buyers acquire property through Stackly?
+
Yes. Our international legal desk assists with foreign national ownership frameworks, Golden Visa programs, cross-border currency exchange, and multi-jurisdictional tax optimization.
What criteria must a property meet to be listed in Stackly's VIP Collection?
+
Listings undergo rigorous architectural appraisal, verified clean title certification, valuation audits, and structural environmental inspections. Only the top 2% of luxury properties qualify.
How does Stackly's mortgage and investment ROI advisory operate?
+
We collaborate directly with premier private private-banking syndicates to secure bespoke jumbo loans, interest-only facilities, and asset-backed financing tailored to high-net-worth balance sheets.
